==============================================================================
tests/Assets/generated-music - four pieces of model-generated music
==============================================================================

These files are NOT third-party recordings or transcriptions. Every one is the
OUTPUT of a model that was run locally by the owner of this repository, from
models published under the Apache-2.0 license. Nothing of the models themselves
is here: the files are a few kilobytes of notes. See THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt.

They exist because a fixture phrase proves a voicing makes a sound, and only a
piece of music proves the bank can carry one. These four were picked from a
listening session in which they were RATED, so the listening tests audition the
General MIDI bank on material whose musical worth was already settled.

WHAT EACH PIECE IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  skytnt-fp32-ordinary.mid   SkyTNT midi-model-tv2o-medium, full precision.
                             About a minute long, three parts: piano, violin
                             and flute. Rated 8/10 - "very ambient ...
                             ethereal". It carries its own program changes and
                             is played EXACTLY as written, with no
                             configuration at all, because "plays any .mid"
                             is the promise the multi-timbral synthesizer
                             makes.

  skytnt-fp32-dense.mid      The same model, a denser piece of about
                             thirty-five seconds: violin, nylon guitar and a
                             string ensemble over a drum part on channel 10.
                             Rated 8/10. Also played exactly as written - and
                             see the note below, because what it asks for on
                             channel 10 is worth knowing about.

  mupt-q8_0-duet-Am-waltz-seed29.abc
                             MuPT-v1-8192-190M, quantized to q8_0. A two-voice
                             waltz in A minor, and the highest-rated piece of
                             either audition at 9.7/10 - heard then as celesta
                             over a mixed chorus, which is how it is voiced
                             here: celesta on the upper voice, choir aahs on
                             the lower one, one distinct voice per part.

  mupt-q8_0-air-Dmix-seed11.abc
                             The same model: a slow single-voice air in D
                             mixolydian, rated 8.5/10. ABC carries no
                             instrument unless the transcriber writes a
                             %%MIDI program directive, and this one does not,
                             so the listening tests choose a voice for it.

A NOTE ON THE DENSE PIECE AND CHANNEL 10
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
skytnt-fp32-dense.mid sends a program change to program 0 on channel 10 before
its drum part, which is what a General MIDI writer normally does: on channel 10
a program change chooses a drum KIT, and program 0 is the standard kit. It is
the only fixture in this repository that exercises that message, so it is also
the one that would catch a synthesizer reading it as "put a piano on channel
10" and losing the drums.
